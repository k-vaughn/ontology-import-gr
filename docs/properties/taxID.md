# taxID

The Tax / Fiscal ID of the gr:BusinessEntity, e.g. the TIN in the US or the CIF/NIF in Spain. It is usually assigned by the country of residence

**Domain**: [BusinessEntity](../classes/BusinessEntity.md) or [schema1:Organization](https://w3id.org/citydata/imported/schema1/Organization) or [schema1:Person](https://w3id.org/citydata/imported/schema1/Person)
